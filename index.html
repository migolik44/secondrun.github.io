The grading guide

Mint

No visible wear under normal light. Battery health 90%+. Full function, original screen. Commands top price.

Good

Light micro-scratches, not felt by nail. Battery health 80–89%. Full function. Most traded devices land here.

Fair

Visible scratches or small chips. Battery health 70–79%, or a replaced non-original part. Fully functional.

Poor

Heavy cosmetic wear, cracked glass, or a functional fault. Sold for parts or deep-discount repair stock.
